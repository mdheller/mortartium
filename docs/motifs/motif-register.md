# Motif Register

The motif register is the intake layer for Mortartium ideas.

A motif is a remembered phrase, symbolic pattern, research suspicion, image, analogy, or cross-tradition recurrence that is worth investigating but is not yet an artifact card, claim, or source-backed dossier.

Motifs are not claims. They are research seeds.

## Motif before artifact

Do not create an artifact card from a loose idea. A motif becomes an artifact card only when it has at least one of the following:

1. A defined object.
2. A defined site.
3. A defined object class.
4. A defined source family.
5. A defined material process with teachable evidence.

Until then, it lives here.

## Motif fields

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Motif ID | Stable identifier, e.g. `MORT-MOTIF-0001`. |
| Name | Short phrase for the motif. |
| Status | seed, active, promoted, parked, rejected. |
| Origin | Remembered source, conversation, image, artifact, text, field observation, etc. |
| Core intuition | What the motif suggests. |
| Layers touched | Material, process, symbolic, ritual, institutional, restoration, reception, etc. |
| Candidate archetypes | Archetypes that may organize the motif. |
| Search targets | Phrases, source families, object classes, or sites to investigate. |
| Promotion rule | What would allow promotion into artifact card, source card, dossier, or claim. |
| Boundary | What is not being claimed. |

## Active motifs

### MORT-MOTIF-0001 — Body-sized column containment

**Status:** Active seed.

**Origin:** Anonymous remembered book source, withheld until identified by Michael Heller.

**Core intuition:** Some column, pillar, cippus, funerary altar, sarcophagus, crypt, or civic-column traditions may preserve an association between vertical architectural form and bodily containment, ash containment, body-scale proportion, or memorial substitution.

**Layers touched:** Material, funerary, architectural, symbolic, institutional, reception.

**Candidate archetypes:** Column-Tomb, Relic-Core, Ruin-as-Body, Threshold / Initiation.

**Search targets:** Roman column-tombs; cippi; funerary altars with ash cavities; columned sarcophagi; pillar tombs; reliquary columns; crypt columns; victory columns with tomb bases; body-proportioned funerary architecture.

**Promotion rule:** Promote only when tied to a specific object, object class, or source family.

**Boundary:** The motif does not claim that all columns contain remains or that column dimensions generally map to a body.

### MORT-MOTIF-0002 — Horror of the bricks / blood brick

**Status:** Active seed.

**Origin:** Anonymous remembered esoteric source or author concerning “the horror of the bricks,” with possible association among red brick, blood, sacrifice, killing, or body-substance.

**Core intuition:** Brick may preserve a layered horror motif: fired earth, red color, blood-earth symbolism, forced labor, kiln violence, sacrifice, foundation killing, or body-substitute construction.

**Layers touched:** Material, furnace/process, labor history, symbolic, ritual, literary/esoteric reception, hard-claim material testing.

**Candidate archetypes:** Royal Furnace, Death-Bind, Weeping Wall, Ruin-as-Body.

**Search targets:** brick color chemistry; red brick iron oxide firing; yellow brick lime clay firing; blood bricks; brick sacrifice; foundation sacrifice; kiln sacrifice; forced labor brickmaking; slavery brickmaking; “horror of the bricks”; esoteric brick symbolism.

**Promotion rule:** Promote into artifact card only when tied to a material class with sources, a source family, a specific site, or a documented tradition.

**Boundary:** The motif does not claim that human blood or human remains made red bricks red.

### MORT-MOTIF-0003 — Organ-color-glass correspondence

**Status:** Active seed.

**Origin:** Remembered Hall/Steiner/esoteric reading lane; initial prompt involved stained glass, organs, color, gallbladder/yellow, and cathedral symbolism.

**Core intuition:** Organ, humor, planet, metal, and color correspondences may intersect with stained glass, halo/aureole symbolism, and sacred light-body architecture.

**Layers touched:** Symbolic anatomy, color theory, stained glass, light-body, humoral medicine, esoteric reception, hard-claim material testing.

**Candidate archetypes:** Light-Body, Bone-to-Color, Royal Furnace, Relic-Core.

**Search targets:** Hall light/color; Hall occult anatomy; Steiner salt/mercury/sulfur; organ-color correspondences; humoral yellow bile; gallbladder/yellow; stained glass paint binders; grisaille; silver stain; bone ash/phosphate glass; relic dust.

**Promotion rule:** Promote symbolic correspondence into theory/dossier when sourced. Promote material claims only with object-specific recipe or conservation evidence.

**Boundary:** Symbolic organ-color correspondence does not prove material organ use.

### MORT-MOTIF-0004 — Mortar as death-bind

**Status:** Active concept.

**Origin:** Conversation and constructed symbolic etymology around `mort-`, `mortarium`, mortar, death, and binding.

**Core intuition:** Mortar can be read as a death-bind: a medium that recomposes burned, broken, inert, mineral, and sometimes organic matter into architectural body.

**Layers touched:** Material, philological, symbolic, masonry, restoration, covenant/joint.

**Candidate archetypes:** Death-Bind, Weeping Wall, Ruin-as-Body.

**Search targets:** mortarium etymology; lime mortar; slaking; carbonation; sacrificial joint; repointing; mortar decay; masonic bond/joint symbolism.

**Promotion rule:** Material side can promote through lime mortar artifact card. Symbolic etymology stays marked as interpretive, not dictionary etymology.

**Boundary:** Mortar does not strictly derive from `mors/mortis`; death-bind is symbolic interpretation.

### MORT-MOTIF-0005 — Weeping wall / structure confesses

**Status:** Active concept.

**Origin:** Masonry weeping discussion: efflorescence, lime bloom, salt migration, damp, calthemites, spalling.

**Core intuition:** Built surfaces can externalize hidden material history through moisture-mediated exudation, staining, salts, cracks, and decay. The wall “confesses” its inner chemistry.

**Layers touched:** Material, restoration, chemistry, symbolic, forensic reading.

**Candidate archetypes:** Weeping Wall, Ruin-as-Body, Death-Bind.

**Search targets:** efflorescence; salt crystallization; lime bloom; calthemite; sulfate attack; spalling; rising damp; conservation moisture mapping.

**Promotion rule:** Promote through sourced artifact cards on lime mortar, calthemites, salt attack, or specific sites.

**Boundary:** Decay does not prove intentional symbolism.
