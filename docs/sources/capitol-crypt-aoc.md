summary: >
  The Architect of the Capitol identifies the Capitol Crypt as a vaulted space beneath the Rotunda, completed in the central section of the Capitol under Charles Bulfinch. The source states that the space has long been called the Crypt because of its resemblance to church crypts, that forty Aquia Creek sandstone Doric columns support the floor above, and that a space below the Crypt had been intended for the remains of George and Martha Washington. It explicitly states that their graves remained at Mount Vernon and that no one has been buried in the Capitol.

## Notes

This is the primary source for the Capitol Crypt entry until more detailed Capitol archival material is added.

Use this source to teach the difference between:

- a crypt-like architectural chamber,
- an unrealized tomb program,
- actual burial or remains presence,
- and later civic-symbolic interpretation.
