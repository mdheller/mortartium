## Working source trail

The current working trail includes:

- National Park Service, **Preservation Brief 2: Repointing Mortar Joints in Historic Masonry Buildings**. Exact official URL/PDF still to pin.
- Repointing reference summaries describing the need to match replacement mortar to historic masonry performance, including permeability, compressive strength, and thermal behavior.
- Lime mortar reference summaries describing lime mortar as lime, aggregate, and water, and noting its role in historic masonry repair.
- Conservation literature on inappropriate Portland cement repair mortars in historic structures.

## Teaching use

Use this source lane to teach:

1. Lime mortar as a material process: calcination, slaking, mixing, carbonation.
2. Mortar joint as a form: the repairable bond between masonry units.
3. Sacrificial joint logic: the joint should often fail before softer historic masonry units fail.
4. Death-Bind as symbolic reading: broken/transformed mineral matter recomposed into architectural body.
5. Non-claim boundary: symbolic death-bind does not prove strict etymology or esoteric intention.
