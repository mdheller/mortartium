# Archetypes

Mortartium uses archetypes as layered research patterns. An archetype is not a claim by itself. It is a reusable lens for identifying material process, artifact form, symbolic operation, institutional authority, and evidence requirements.

Artifacts may belong to multiple archetypes. Layering is expected.

## Archetype index

| Archetype | Core question | Typical material | Primary risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Death-Bind | What binds fragments into a durable body? | Mortar, lime, glue, resin, plaster, oath, joint | Mistaking symbolic reading for etymology or material proof |
| Weeping Wall | What hidden chemistry becomes visible at the surface? | Salt, lime, sulfate, carbonate, water, stain, spall | Overreading decay as intentional symbolism |
| Light-Body | How does transformed matter carry light or sanctity? | Glass, pigment, gold, lead, enamel, halo, aureole | Inferring material recipe from color symbolism |
| Relic-Core | How do remains anchor sacred space? | Bone, hair, blood, cloth, tomb, reliquary, altar | Inferring remains in construction media from relic presence |
| Inscribed Skin | How does body become writing surface? | Parchment, vellum, leather, ink, pigment, gold | Ignoring ordinary craft facts in favor of only symbolism |
| Bone-to-Color | How do skeletal remains become color, vessel, or medium? | Bone black, bone ash, ivory, phosphate, cremains | Inferring human remains from any calcium/phosphate signal |
| Royal Furnace | What does controlled fire destroy and remake? | Kiln, forge, lime kiln, glass furnace, metal, brick | Treating all furnace work as alchemical intentionality |
| Threshold / Initiation | What artifact organizes passage through death, secrecy, or rebirth? | Gate, arch, veil, crypt, lodge room, labyrinth | Assuming institutional mystery-school continuity without evidence |
| Ruin-as-Body | How does fragment, damage, or repair become witness? | Ruin, spolia, restoration layer, inscription, patched wall | Treating restoration as neutral or original fabric |
| Sounding Remains | How does transformed matter become voice? | Skin drum, gut string, bone flute, horn, bell, wood | Treating functional material history as ritual proof by default |

## Reading protocol

For each artifact:

1. Identify the material substrate.
2. Identify the destructive process.
3. Identify the stabilization or binding process.
4. Identify the visible artifact.
5. Identify hidden layers: chemistry, repair, substrate, relic presence, contamination, symbolic code.
6. Identify symbolic operations, if supported.
7. Identify institutional authority.
8. Assign claim grade.
9. Record non-claim boundaries.
10. Define open tests.

## Layering rule

An artifact may be read symbolically with confidence while remaining materially unproven at another layer.

Example: a rose window can be established as a glass/light artifact and plausibly read as a light-body symbol. That does not establish that organ-derived or human-remains-derived materials were used in its colorants.

Example: a relic altar may be established as remains-centered sacred architecture. That does not establish that relic matter was mixed into its mortar or plaster.

Example: a lime mortar joint may materially enact calcination, slaking, binding, carbonation, and repair. That does not prove intentional Masonic doctrine in the joint.
