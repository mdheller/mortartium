## Reading

Brick is a prime Mortartium object because it is earth destructively transformed by fire into a modular body-unit. It is then bound by mortar into walls, roads, kilns, temples, factories, prisons, houses, and graves.

The remembered esoteric phrase “the horror of the bricks” opens a legitimate investigation. It may point to one or more layers:

- **Material layer:** red and yellow brick color chemistry.
- **Furnace layer:** kiln fire as destructive transformation.
- **Labor layer:** coerced work, slavery, punishment, or death-producing construction regimes.
- **Ritual layer:** sacrifice, foundation killing, kiln legends, blood offerings, or body-substitute traditions.
- **Symbolic layer:** red brick as blood-earth or fired body.
- **Hard-claim layer:** actual use of blood, remains, or body-derived matter in brick production.

## Claim boundary

Mortartium does not claim that human blood or human remains were used to make red bricks red.

Mortartium does claim that brick color, brickmaking, kiln violence, forced labor, ritual sacrifice, and esoteric horror motifs are legitimate research targets under the Royal Furnace and Death-Bind archetypes.
